Frequently Answered Queries

Instant clarity on deliveries, hearth cook timings, courier routing, and cold-chain standards.

Every NAURIO order offers high-frequency telemetry. You can watch your chef fire the order, monitor temperature readings, and observe courier GPS coordinates with satellite precision directly in our tracking portal.

To maintain the integrity of artisanal preparation, partner kitchens initiate preparation quickly. You have a 60-second hearth firing window from the moment payment completes to cancel or adjust dish ingredients directly in your app. Once oven preparation begins, cancellations incur a culinary preparation charge.

Payments processed via UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, CRED) are routed through our direct webhook settlement corridor. Validated refunds reflect in your bank account within 15 minutes. Credit card and Net Banking reversals are cleared by banking gateways within 2 to 4 business days.

We operate on an uncompromising standard. If your food core temperature registers below 60°C (for hot courses) upon delivery, use the temperature discrepancy button inside the ticket form below. After simple thermal probe or condensation seal validation, we grant a 100% instant refund in Crave Coins and notify the culinary executive.

Address modifications within an 800-meter radius can be executed on-the-fly via the Live Courier Concierge chat. For longer distances, tap the Concierge Hotline (+91 1800-NAURIO) immediately so our dispatch manager can adjust the thermal route profile.

Artisanal promotional codes require minimum cart thresholds (excluding delivery & eco-packaging charges). Some partner chefs or Michelin-curated menus do not participate in blanket discounting. Ensure your code matches specific culinary houses or contact concierge for corporate validation.
