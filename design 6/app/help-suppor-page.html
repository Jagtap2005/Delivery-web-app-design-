How can I track my order live? expand_more
You can view live GPS tracking, kitchen prep updates, and delivery partner route directly under the Orders tab or tap the persistent card on top of this screen.
How long does a refund take to reflect? expand_more
UPI and Crave Coin refunds reflect instantly (under 60 seconds). Netbanking and credit cards typically settle within 2–4 business days depending on your bank partner.
What if I receive food that is not warm? expand_more
Our Thermal-Lock guarantee provides a 100% refund or instant re-fire. Simply upload a picture of the container seal below or alert your concierge within 15 minutes of delivery.
Can I cancel or modify an ongoing order? expand_more
Orders can be modified within 90 seconds of placement. Once the kitchen accepts and begins preparation, cancellations may incur a nominal restaurant prep fee.

Some gourmet partner restaurants have exclusive fixed pricing that excludes platform-wide discounts. Check minimum cart value requirements in your cart breakdown.
