Atelier Lineage

Sanctuary of Ferment, Hearth & Heritage

Founded under the culinary tutelage of Michelin-alumnus Chef Marco Valenti, Osteria Mozza Vetta revives centuries-old Campanian craft. Every dough ball is hand-kneaded with stone-milled ancient grains and slow cold-fermented for 48 hours to yield an airy, caramelized cornicione.

Our produce travels directly from single-estate harvest purveyors: sun-ripened San Marzano DOP tomatoes from the volcanic foot of Mount Vesuvius, whole-milk Mozzarella di Bufala flown weekly from Caserta, and unpasteurized Sicilian cold-pressed monocultivar oils.
